# Brand Asset / Logo Provenance

This revision switches the gameplay from invented wordmark placeholders to real brand
marks loaded as individual SVG assets from the Simple Icons CDN (`simple-icons@16.30.0`).

Simple Icons states that the collection itself is released under CC0, while individual
brand marks may still be subject to trademark rights, licenses, and brand guidelines.
The project therefore treats these marks as third-party trademarks and does not imply
endorsement or affiliation.

Source collection:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
CDN:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.30.0/icons/

Before using this build for a commercial/public event, review the individual brand
guidelines and permissions relevant to the intended use. See the Simple Icons
disclaimer for the distinction between the icon artwork license and trademark rights.

The game has an on-error fallback to the original wordmark so a missing CDN icon cannot
break a round.
